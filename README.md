# Suadat Bin Iqbal
I'm just a guy who loves mathematics, programming, music, films, anime, and video games. I tend to overthink everything, get lost in random thoughts, and spend hours exploring ideas that probably started from a single question.
I'm fascinated by Japan and Japanese culture, its atmosphere, traditions, and stories. My favourite anime movie is *Kimi no Na wa* (*Your Name*), and my favourite anime are *Vinland Saga* and *Attack on Titan*. I love stories that leave you thinking long after they're over, especially ones with complex characters and deeper meanings.
When I'm not coding or studying mathematics, I'm probably listening to music, watching a film, or playing games. I absolutely love Minecraft, whether it's exploring, building, or getting lost in its world. I play War Thunder too. There's something about immersing yourself in a game and forgetting about everything else for a while that I enjoy.
